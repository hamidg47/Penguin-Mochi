MOCHI PENGUIN - Windows desktop companion

Open Mochi Penguin from the Start menu. No Python installation is needed.
Right-click the penguin (or his tray icon) for food, toys and activities.

Right-click Mochi > Activities & cameos > Madagascar for a smile-and-wave commando routine:
salute, binocular lookout, tiptoe and belly slide. It can also happen during
spontaneous play. Quiet time pauses automatic cameos; Escape stops an activity.

Mochi chooses his own games, visits and rest breaks. Allow a minute or two
between activities. Quiet time pauses spontaneous play. Low energy means
he needs sleep; when hungry, he catches lunch before playing.

Feed him yourself using Feed / place food, or let him catch lunch when hungry.
Food is unlimited. A failed hungry fishing trip ends with a packed fish lunch.
Home & rest includes Remove snowman; completed snowmen also melt away.
Activities & cameos includes a visiting Skipper-inspired captain.

Start with Windows is optional in the right-click menu. Press Escape to
release the cursor during a cursor game. Use Quit companion to close him.
Only one installed copy runs at a time.

Save data: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mochi Penguin
Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > Mochi Penguin > Uninstall.
Uninstall keeps your pet's saved progress. Close the pet before updating.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11. This release is not code-signed.
An older Python-script version should be quit before opening this app.

TREASURE HUNT: Activities & cameos > Treasure hunt. Mochi follows a map,
checks clues, digs, and carries a pearl, star or crystal back to the igloo.
Keepsakes are saved. Hunts can happen spontaneously and respect quiet time.
Drag Mochi or choose Stop current activity to cancel a hunt.
