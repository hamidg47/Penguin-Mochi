# Mochi Penguin 🐧

**A little penguin companion for your Windows desktop.**

Mochi Penguin is a desktop companion who plays, explores, eats, rests, and occasionally surprises you with spontaneous activities. Interact with him through the right-click menu, give him food, play games, and discover his special activities and cameos.

## Installation

1. Install Mochi Penguin using the Windows installer.
2. Open **Mochi Penguin** from the Windows Start menu.
3. Right-click Mochi or his system tray icon to explore the available options.

**Requirements:** 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11. Python does not need to be installed.

> **Note:** This release is not code-signed. If Windows displays a security warning, verify that the installer came from the official project release before proceeding.

## Features

### 🐧 An Independent Desktop Companion

Mochi has his own routine. He chooses when to play, visit, and rest, with pauses between activities. His behaviour responds to his energy and hunger.

* **Food and feeding:** Feed Mochi using *Feed / place food*, or let him catch lunch when he gets hungry. Food is unlimited. If a hungry fishing trip fails, he finishes with a packed fish lunch.
* **Rest and recovery:** Low energy means Mochi needs sleep.
* **Spontaneous activities:** Mochi can initiate activities on his own.
* **Quiet time:** Pause automatic cameos and spontaneous play when you want a quieter desktop.

### 🎮 Activities & Cameos

Open **Activities & cameos** from Mochi's right-click menu to discover his special routines.

**Madagascar**

Mochi performs a smile-and-wave commando routine featuring a salute, binocular lookout, tiptoe, and belly slide. This routine can also occur during spontaneous play.

**Treasure Hunt**

Choose **Activities & cameos → Treasure hunt** to send Mochi on an adventure. He follows a map, investigates clues, digs for treasure, and carries a pearl, star, or crystal back to his igloo.

* Treasure hunts can also happen spontaneously.
* Keepsakes are saved between sessions.
* Quiet time pauses spontaneous activities.
* Drag Mochi or select *Stop current activity* to cancel a hunt.

**Visiting Captain**

Mochi can also welcome a visiting, Skipper-inspired captain.

### ☃️ Home & Rest

The *Home & rest* menu includes **Remove snowman**. Completed snowmen also melt away on their own.

### 🖱️ Desktop Interaction

* Right-click Mochi to access food, toys, activities, and other options.
* Press **Escape** to release the cursor during a cursor game or stop an activity where supported.
* Choose **Stop current activity** when available to cancel an ongoing activity.
* Select **Quit companion** to close Mochi.
* Only one installed copy runs at a time.

## Settings & Saved Progress

**Start with Windows** is optional and can be enabled or disabled from Mochi's right-click menu.

Mochi stores his saved data in:

```text
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Mochi Penguin
```

Uninstalling the application preserves your pet's saved progress.

**Before updating:** Close the running companion before installing the new version.

To uninstall Mochi Penguin, open:

**Windows Settings → Apps → Mochi Penguin → Uninstall**

## Troubleshooting

**Mochi does not open**

Check whether another installed copy is already running. Only one copy can run at a time. Also, close any older Python-script version before launching the Windows application.

**Mochi is busy with an activity**

Wait for the activity to finish, or use *Stop current activity* where available. Press **Escape** to release the cursor during a cursor game.

**I want Mochi to be quieter**

Enable *Quiet time* to pause automatic cameos and spontaneous play.
